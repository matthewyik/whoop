Formulas used on this page
Model settings / assumptions
Measured / returned by WHOOP
  • Recovery score, HRV, resting HR, SpO₂ and skin temperature.
  • Sleep stages, sleep performance, sleep consistency, sleep efficiency and WHOOP sleep-need components.
  • Cycle strain, heart-rate data and energy in kilojoules.
  • Current body weight from body.weight_kilogram.
Modeled by this dashboard
  • Training-intensity and nap recommendations from your configurable thresholds.
  • Calorie target from recent WHOOP energy expenditure + goal-paced surplus.
  • Protein and per-meal targets.
  • Target-weight trajectory and calorie/recovery associations from your manual logs.
  • “Muscle gain” is not directly measured; without body-composition data this dashboard tracks body-weight gain.
Update the dashboard with a new WHOOP pull
When opened through the included Windows launcher, this dashboard automatically reloads whoop_data.json on startup and every 5 minutes. Your scheduled task keeps that JSON fresh.
